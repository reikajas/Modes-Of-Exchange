A.

The epochal breakthrough in our understanding of the social formation in clan societies came from Marcel Mauss. He analyzed it not in terms of productive forces or means of production, but rather of exchange. This was not commodity exchange, but rather the reciprocal exchange of gift/counter-gift. I call this
Mode of Exchange A
. This kind of exchange is comprised of three rules: one must give gifts, one must accept gifts, and one must reciprocate for gifts received. These rules are not something that people invented. They are instead imposed by a ‘magical power’ that people are compelled to obey. The social formation of clan society is created through this principle of exchange. For example, the form of kinship is established through reciprocal exchanges in which one gives one’s daughter or son to another community and then receives in turn a reciprocal return gift. In this sense, the clan society social formation was established by exchange in this broad sense, and this is what constitutes its true economic base.

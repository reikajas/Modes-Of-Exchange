The Modes of Exchange, as defined by Kojin Karatani, are defined as follows: Mode of Exchange A, also known as Reciprocity, Mode of Exchange B, also known as Redistribution, Mode of Exchange C, also known as Market Exchange, and Mode of Exchange D, also known as Free Gift ☻.
